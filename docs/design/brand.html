Construction

The cursor sits on the ring at 32° below 3 o’clock. The ring opens only far enough to clear it by a third of the stroke on each side, so it reads as one O rather than a spinner or a C.

Monochrome on dark

The ring takes the text color of whatever it sits on. The cursor stays amber in every form.

Small sizes

At 32px and below the ring thickens and the cursor grows, so the mark keeps its shape at Finder and list sizes.
